# Fonts

Self-hosted so the app works offline. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

- **Grandstander** (bold), by Tyler Finck. Display face: the name, titles, «¡Fin!».
- **Andika** (regular, bold), by SIL International. Reading face, designed for people who are learning to read.

The files are the latin and latin-ext subsets from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com), downloaded on 2026-11-10.
